1. Logging in and out

  1. Open the clinic system address in your browser. The login page appears.
  2. Type your username and password, then press Log in.
  3. After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for a while - ask the administrator if that happens.
  4. The Dashboard shows the menu and today's appointments. The Reports item only appears for administrators.
  5. When you finish, always press Exit. It signs you out and returns to the login page. If you are idle for a long time the system signs you out by itself.

2. Booking an appointment

  1. Choose Register Appointment in the menu.
  2. Existing patient: type part of the name or phone number, press Search, then press Select next to the right person.
  3. New patient: choose "New patient" and fill in the form. First and last name use letters only. The phone number must be a mobile number like 0771234567 (07 followed by 8 digits). The address is optional but needs at least 5 characters when entered.
  4. Pick the dentist and the treatment. The page shows the dentist's working hours and how long the treatment takes.
  5. Pick the date (today or later) and the time (between 08:00 and 18:00, in steps of 15 minutes). Times that are already taken for that dentist are listed under the form.
  6. Press Book appointment. A confirmation shows the new appointment number, for example APT-0007. Give this number to the patient.

A dentist cannot have two appointments at the same time. If the slot is taken, choose another time.

3. Finding an appointment

  1. Choose Search.
  2. Type the appointment number (format APT-0001) and press Search.
  3. The page shows the date, time, status, patient, dentist, treatment and notes.

4. Changing the status

On the search result you can press the buttons that apply:

  • Confirm - the patient confirmed the visit.
  • Mark completed - the treatment was done. Only completed appointments can be billed.
  • Mark no-show - the patient did not come.
  • Cancel appointment - frees the time slot. You are asked to confirm first.

5. Billing and printing a receipt

  1. Complete the appointment first (see section 4).
  2. Choose Bill, type the appointment number and press Load.
  3. Choose the billing type: Standard, Senior citizen discount (patient aged 60 or over) or Insurance (you may enter the cover percentage).
  4. Press Calculate and issue bill. The receipt appears: treatment price + consultation fee - discount.
  5. Press Print receipt. In the print window only the receipt is printed.
  6. When the patient pays, choose the payment method and press Mark as paid.

An appointment can only be billed once. If you try again, the system opens the existing receipt.

6. Reports (administrators)

  1. Choose Reports, pick a report and the dates (or year and month) and press Show report.
  2. You see a bar chart and a table. Download CSV saves the table for Excel.

7. Messages and what to do

  • "Your session has expired" - log in again.
  • "Cannot reach the server" - check your network; if it continues, tell the administrator.
  • Red text under a field - correct that field. The same rules are checked again by the server.
  • "You do not have permission" - the action needs an administrator.
